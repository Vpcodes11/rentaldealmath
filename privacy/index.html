Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information RentalDealMath ("we", "us") collects when you use rentaldealmath.com, and how we use it.

Numbers you enter into calculators

Our calculators run entirely in your web browser. The property figures you type in are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are visited, how long people stay, browser and device type, and approximate location (country or city). Google Analytics uses cookies. Visitors in the UK, EU and EEA are asked for consent first, and analytics cookies are not set unless you accept. We use this only to improve the site. You can opt out at any time with Google\'s browser add-on.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This does not change how our calculators work.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The calculators still work with cookies turned off. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access or delete personal information we hold. Contact us to make a request.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
