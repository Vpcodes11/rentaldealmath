MTD quarterly update deadlines for landlords

Under Making Tax Digital for Income Tax you send HMRC a summary of your rental income and expenses four times a year. The deadlines are the same every year, so once you know them you can plan around them.

Standard update periods and deadlines

The four deadlines

Quarterly updates are due on 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May. Each one is due about a month after the period it covers ends. The tax return that finishes the year (sometimes called the final declaration) is still due on 31 January after the tax year ends, the same date as Self Assessment today.

2026/27 dates for landlords who started in April 2026

If your qualifying income was over £50,000 in 2024/25, you joined on 6 April 2026. Your first update was due on 7 August 2026 and the second on 7 November 2026. The next ones are 7 February 2027 (covering 6 April 2026 to 5 January 2027) and 7 May 2027 (the full year). Your 2026/27 tax return is due by 31 January 2028.

2027/28 dates for landlords who start in April 2027

If your qualifying income in 2025/26 is over £30,000, you start on 6 April 2027. Your first update is due on 7 August 2027, then 7 November 2027, 7 February 2028 and 7 May 2028, with the tax return due by 31 January 2029.

Each update is cumulative

Every update covers the tax year so far, not just the latest three months. So the November update repeats the figures from the August one, plus anything new and any corrections. That is helpful: if you spot a mistake in an earlier quarter, you fix it in the next update rather than resubmitting the old one.

One update per income source

Landlords with UK property and foreign property send a separate update for each. If you are also self-employed, each business sends its own update too. Two UK rental properties count as one UK property business, so they go in a single update.

Can you file early?

Yes. You can send an update any time after the period ends. Many landlords do it in the first week after the quarter closes, while the bank statements are fresh.

Frequently asked questions

When are MTD quarterly updates due?

7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May each tax year, for the standard periods ending 5 July, 5 October, 5 January and 5 April.

Is the 31 January deadline still there?

Yes. The tax return that completes the year is due by 31 January after the end of the tax year, and that is also when any tax is due.

Do the deadlines change if I use calendar quarters?

No. The periods end on 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March instead, but the deadlines stay on the 7th of August, November, February and May.

What is the next deadline?

For landlords already in Making Tax Digital, the next quarterly update is due on 7 February 2027, covering 6 April 2026 to 5 January 2027.
