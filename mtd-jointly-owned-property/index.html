Making Tax Digital for jointly owned rental property

Many buy-to-lets are owned by couples, siblings or friends. Making Tax Digital looks at each owner separately, which changes both whether you are in scope and what you report.

Each owner is assessed on their own share

For the threshold test, you count only your share of the gross rent. A property that lets for £50,000 a year owned 50/50 gives each owner £25,000 of qualifying income. That is under £30,000, so neither owner is in from April 2027 on that property alone, but both are over £20,000 for April 2028.

Married couples and civil partners

Income from property held jointly by spouses or civil partners is normally split 50/50 for tax. If you actually own it in different shares, you can tell HMRC with a declaration of beneficial interests (Form 17), and then the actual shares apply.

Each in-scope owner sends their own updates

If both owners are in Making Tax Digital, each sends quarterly updates for their own share. If only one is in scope, only that person sends updates. The other carries on with a normal Self Assessment return.

The joint-owner simplification

HMRC has said joint owners can choose to send only their share of income in the quarterly updates and give their share of expenses at year end in the tax return. That saves splitting every cost each quarter, which is often the hardest part when one person pays the bills.

Frequently asked questions

Do I count the whole rent if I own half?

No. Only your share counts, both for the threshold and for your updates.

Does my partner have to join MTD too?

Only if their own qualifying income is over the threshold. Each person is assessed separately.

Do joint owners need to split expenses every quarter?

Not necessarily. HMRC allows joint owners to report just their share of income quarterly and their share of expenses at year end.

What if we do not own it 50/50?

Married couples and civil partners are taxed 50/50 by default unless they submit Form 17 to HMRC. Other joint owners use their actual shares.
