The £30,000 Making Tax Digital threshold: are you in from April 2027?

The second wave of Making Tax Digital for Income Tax starts on 6 April 2027 and is much bigger than the first. If your rent alone is over £30,000 a year, it probably includes you.

Who is in from April 2027

You must use Making Tax Digital from 6 April 2027 if your qualifying income on your 2025/26 tax return is over £30,000. Qualifying income means gross rent plus self-employment turnover, before expenses. That 2025/26 return is due by 31 January 2027, so most people will know their figure by then.

Why this catches so many landlords

Because the test uses rent before costs, a landlord with a mortgaged buy-to-let portfolio can be well over £30,000 while making little profit. Two or three typical rental properties can be enough.

What to do before 6 April 2027

  • Check your number. Add up gross rent (your share for joint property) and any self-employment turnover for 2025/26. The MTD checker does this for you.
  • Pick your records. Either MTD software, or a spreadsheet plus bridging software.
  • Set up your categories. Record income and costs in HMRC's property categories from 6 April 2027.
  • Sign up on GOV.UK before your first update is due on 7 August 2027. You or your agent can sign up.

The first-year penalty relief does not carry over

Landlords who joined in April 2026 had a soft landing: no penalty points for late quarterly updates in 2026/27. That relief was for 2026/27 only. Reports from accountancy bodies say people joining in April 2027 should expect the normal points system from their first update, so plan to be on time.

And then £20,000 from April 2028

If you are between £20,000 and £30,000, you are likely to join on 6 April 2028, based on your 2026/27 income.

Frequently asked questions

Which tax year decides if I join in April 2027?

Your 2025/26 Self Assessment return. If qualifying income on it is over £30,000, you start on 6 April 2027.

Will HMRC write to me?

HMRC writes to people it expects to be in scope, but it is your responsibility to check and sign up.

I am at £29,000. Am I in?

No, it must be over £30,000. But over £20,000 on your 2026/27 return brings you in from April 2028.

Do I still file a tax return?

Yes. You send four quarterly updates and then a tax return by 31 January after the tax year.
