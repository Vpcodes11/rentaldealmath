Frequently asked questions

When does Making Tax Digital start for landlords?

From 6 April 2026 for qualifying income over £50,000, 6 April 2027 for over £30,000 and 6 April 2028 for over £20,000.

How often do landlords report under MTD?

Four quarterly updates a year, due 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May, then a tax return by 31 January.
