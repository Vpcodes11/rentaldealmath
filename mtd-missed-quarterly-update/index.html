Missed an MTD quarterly update? What happens now

Plenty of landlords missed the first Making Tax Digital deadlines in 2026. If you are one of them, the news is mostly good for this year, but the rules tighten from April 2027.

2026/27: no penalty points for late quarterly updates

For the 2026/27 tax year, HMRC is not giving penalty points for late quarterly updates. This first-year soft landing covers all four updates due on 7 August 2026, 7 November 2026, 7 February 2027 and 7 May 2027.

What the soft landing does not cover

  • You still have to send every update. HMRC needs them before you can file your tax return.
  • The 2026/27 tax return, due by 31 January 2028, gets penalty points if it is late.
  • Tax paid late still runs interest, and late payment penalties apply after the first-year grace period.

How penalty points work from 2027/28

Each missed deadline earns one point. Once you reach four points with quarterly updates, you get a £200 penalty, and another £200 for each later missed deadline while you stay at the limit. Points expire after a period of filing on time.

How to catch up

  • Bring your records up to date for the tax year so far.
  • Send the latest update. Because updates are cumulative, one update to the latest period end covers everything since 6 April.
  • Set a reminder a week before 7 February and 7 May.

Haven't signed up yet?

If you should have joined in April 2026 and haven't, sign up on GOV.UK now and send your catch-up update. If you are not sure you are in scope, use the MTD checker.

Frequently asked questions

Will I get a fine for missing the August or November 2026 update?

No penalty points apply for late quarterly updates in 2026/27, but you must still send them.

When do penalty points start for quarterly updates?

From the 2027/28 tax year. One point per missed deadline, and a £200 penalty at four points.

Do I have to resend every missed quarter?

Updates are cumulative, so the latest update covers the year to date. Your software will show which obligations are still open.

Does the soft landing apply if I join in April 2027?

No. The relief is for 2026/27 only.
