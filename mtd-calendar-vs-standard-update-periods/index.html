Calendar quarters or standard update periods for MTD?

Making Tax Digital uses quarters that match the tax year by default, ending on the 5th of the month. You can choose quarters ending at month end instead, which many landlords find easier to match to bank statements.

Standard update periods

These run from 6 April and end on 5 July, 5 October, 5 January and 5 April. They line up exactly with the tax year, so the fourth update covers the whole year.

Calendar update periods

These end on 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March. They match monthly bank statements and many agents' rent statements. Your software lines everything up with the tax year when you do your year-end return.

Deadlines are the same

Whichever you choose, quarterly updates are due on 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May.

Which should you pick?

  • Choose calendar if you work from monthly statements and want each quarter to be three whole months.
  • Choose standard if you want each update to match the tax year exactly, with no adjustment at year end.

How to choose

You make the choice in your MTD software, usually when you sign up or before your first update. Check your software's settings if you want to change it later.

Frequently asked questions

Do calendar quarters change the deadlines?

No. Updates are still due on the 7th of August, November, February and May.

Are updates cumulative with calendar quarters too?

Yes. Each update covers the tax year so far.

Is one option better for tax?

No. The choice only changes how your quarters are cut. Your tax for the year is worked out the same way.

Can my spreadsheet handle either?

Yes, if it totals by date range. The MTD Landlord Spreadsheet lets you choose standard or calendar periods.
