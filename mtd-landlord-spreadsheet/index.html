How to use it with Making Tax Digital

From your start date, HMRC expects digital records and a quarterly update with totals for each category. Spreadsheets are allowed as long as they are linked to HMRC through bridging software. This template does the record keeping and the sums: you add each payment as it happens, and the Quarterly updates tab always shows the year-to-date figures in HMRC's categories. When a deadline comes round, your bridging software or accountant reads those totals and sends them.

Why landlords prefer a spreadsheet

Most landlords with one to ten properties have few transactions a month. Full accounting software can feel heavy for that, and many accountants are happy to receive a tidy spreadsheet. Keeping the records yourself also means you can see your rental profit per property at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Does this spreadsheet send my updates to HMRC?

No. HMRC requires updates to be sent from MTD-compatible software. Keep your records in this spreadsheet and use bridging software (or your accountant) to send the totals from the Quarterly updates tab.

Which HMRC categories does it use?

The UK property categories in HMRC's quarterly update direction: total rent, other property income, lease premiums, reverse premiums, rent rates insurance and ground rents, repairs and maintenance, non-residential and residential finance costs, legal management and professional fees, cost of services, travel, and other allowable expenses.

Can I use it for jointly owned property?

Yes. Enter each property's full figures and your ownership share once. Every total is calculated on your share.

Does it work in Google Sheets or on a Mac?

Yes. It works in Excel for Windows and Mac, Google Sheets, Numbers and LibreOffice. It uses standard formulas only, with no macros.

How many properties and transactions does it hold?

The full version holds 20 properties and 2,000 transaction rows per tax year. Start a fresh copy each tax year.

What if the categories or rules change?

Buyers get updated versions free through their Gumroad library for as long as we publish the template.
